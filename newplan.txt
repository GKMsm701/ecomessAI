When safe surplus food remains, the platform records its quantity, type, location, and available time window.
 An automated matching engine then identifies suitable NGOs, relief organizations, or other recipients by considering factors such as quantity requirement, distance, urgency, food requirements, and pickup capacity.
 The platform can also recommend an efficient pickup/delivery route for the assigned agent. 
Unsuitable food that cannot be safely redistributed can instead be directed towards appropriate recovery options such as composting or biogas, subject to applicable safety requirements. The platform therefore creates a closed-loop system that not only facilitates food redistribution but also uses historical data and feedback to continuously improve future food preparation and reduce avoidable waste.  

The platform works as a middleman, coordinating the entire process from food preparation planning to surplus redistribution. 
1. Food Sources The platform supports two major categories: Institutional Kitchens Student hostels Working/staff hostels Schools Colleges Midday Meal Scheme kitchens Hospitals and other large institutional kitchens Other mass-feeding facilities Private / Commercial Kitchens Canteens Working women's hostels Restaurants Catering services Cafeterias Cloud/community kitchens Other large-scale food preparation units 
2. AI-Based Demand Prediction Before food is prepared, the system predicts the expected food requirement. It can consider: Expected number of people Historical consumption Previous surplus/waste Menu Day/date Consumption patterns Other available kitchen data The system can then recommend: How much food should be prepared? This helps prevent surplus from being created unnecessarily.
 3. Surplus Food Registration If safe food remains after serving, the kitchen can list it on the platform. The listing contains: Food type Quantity available Preparation time Location Safe consumption time window Pickup availability We specifically use the term "surplus food" for food that is still safe and suitable for redistribution.
 4. Automated Smart Matching NGOs and relief organizations can submit their requirements, such as: "Need 100 meals within 2 hours." Instead of making the NGO manually search through every available kitchen, the platform's matching engine automatically evaluates available surplus sources. It considers factors such as: Quantity match Distance Urgency/time remaining Food requirements Recipient capacity Pickup availability The system then recommends an appropriate source or combination of sources.
 5. Multiple-Source Allocation A particularly useful feature is when one kitchen cannot satisfy the complete requirement. For example: NGO requirement = 500 meals Kitchen A = 200 meals Kitchen B = 180 meals Kitchen C = 120 meals The system can identify that the requirement can be fulfilled by combining the three available sources and recommend an allocation. This makes the platform more than a simple "donation listing" application. 
6. Route Optimization Once a surplus allocation is confirmed, the platform assigns the pickup/delivery process to a platform agent. The system can recommend an efficient route based on: Pickup locations Recipient location Distance Time constraints Multiple pickup points Thus, the system covers not only finding food, but also moving it efficiently. 
7. Disaster and Emergency Support The platform can also support sudden food requirements during: Floods Natural disasters Emergency relief operations Temporary shelters Large-scale community emergencies For example, a relief organization can submit an urgent requirement, and the system can aggregate suitable surplus from multiple nearby kitchens and recommend an allocation and route. 8. Rewards and Reports The platform can generate monthly reports for participating kitchens showing: Food prepared Estimated consumption Surplus generated Surplus redistributed Waste reduction trends Donations made Institutions can also receive points/rewards and recognition for consistently reducing avoidable waste and redistributing safe surplus food. from the abstract derive point by point
