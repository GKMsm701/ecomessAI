EcoMESS AI:an smart an AI-Powered Smart Food Waste Reduction and Redistribution Ecosystem is an integrated platform that helps institutional kitchens (Hostels, Mid-day Meal Schemes etc. ) and food processing units predict food demand, detect surplus and quality risks, and intelligently distribute excess food before it becomes waste.

-The system uses AI-based demand forecasting to estimate future food requirements and identify likely surplus. in hostel
-combines expiry tracking and basic computer-vision quality checks to monitor food that may no longer be suitable for redistribution. When safe surplus is identified, the platform automatically matches it with suitable NGOs, shelters, food banks, or community kitchens based on food type, quantity, capacity, availability, and predicted local need in ngo to hostels/food processing units
-A logistics module then suggests an efficient pickup/delivery route, 
- feedback system records the quality and quantity experience after redistribution
- The platform also provides multilingual interfaces so kitchen staff can easily report surplus in Hindi or regional languages.
-a smart dashboard tracks food saved, waste avoided, estimated cost savings, redistribution impact, and sustainability metrics, helping institutions make better procurement and production decisions.
